## Screens met

`COMMAND` ×30, `FIGHT` ×30, `MODIFIER_SELECT` ×13, `TARGET_SELECT` ×8, `CONFIRM` ×6, `PARTY/MODIFIER` ×4 (+ `:options` ×4), `PARTY/FAINT_SWITCH` ×3 (+ `:options` ×3), `PARTY/POST_BATTLE_SWITCH` ×2 (+ `:options` ×1), `TITLE` ×2. **No screen Claude did not understand; no modal/form mode; no `timed_out`, no `stuck`, no auto-advance cap.** Every #14 budget and #13 threshold went unused on a healthy run.

## The one error

`select_option("Revive")` → `ambiguous`: Revive was both a free reward (row 1) and a shop item (row 2). Claude recovered with `read_menu` + `press RIGHT` + `press ACTION` — the raw escape hatch, used as designed. It asked for a way to name an option by position; `select_option` now takes `index` (the option's `i` as `read_menu` returned it this call), and `ambiguous` echoes the matches with their indices.

## On battle verbs

A turn is `select_option("Fight")` + `select_option(move)` (+ target in doubles): 60 of the 98 `select_option` calls were COMMAND/FIGHT pairs. A `use_move(name)` verb would have cut ~30 calls (28 %) and roughly that share of the $2.07. The soak does not force the decision: the run was affordable as is, and the cost driver is context growth per turn, which a verb halves but does not remove. Left for whoever wants a cheaper run; not blocking v1.
